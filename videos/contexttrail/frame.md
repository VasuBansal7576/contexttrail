# Frame direction

Use the product's Instrument Serif display face and Geist Sans reading face, with local font files. Warm paper is the principal ground. Blue and rust identify chapters, quotations and the final invitation. Thin rules and generous margins follow the authored A Place for the Question interface.

Seven scenes form one 76.97-second narrated story. See STORYBOARD.md for actual audio timings. Every scene has one clear subject: the question, the search, an inspected passage, three clip samples, the saved case, the watch baseline, or the next question.

Actual app screenshots retain their source text and dates. Scene 3 intentionally crops and slowly pushes into the readable source screenshot; its clipping-container information findings are expected. Scene 4 plays the owner's supplied local clip at the 20.067-second offset. It never presents the historical source lead as a proven match.

The pointing hand comes from the product illustration. No decorative stock footage, invented result cards, gradient blooms or fake evidence. Captions and the interactive companion preserve the exact narration. Respect reduced motion in the companion.

The canonical composition is index.html with compositions/scene1.html through scene7.html. Each scene owns a paused GSAP timeline registered under its composition ID. The root schedules the scenes and voice assets. Source evidence, budgets and product acceptance belong to the app's docs and real-run record, not this design file.
