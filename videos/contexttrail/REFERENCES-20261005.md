## Jev launch

[Diogo Almeida's original launch post](https://x.com/CompleteSkeptic/status/2099925682726002904), posted 15 September 2026. Player duration 176.125 seconds. Studied opening, founder identification, explanatory type, terminal comparison, later explanation and final disclaimer. Also watched the product-reveal section in playback.

- 0 seconds: direct founder shot, warm physical setting, deliberately informal shirt.
- 6 seconds: side angle and a small technical biography box. Founder identity is part of the argument.
- 21 seconds: a large question beside the speaker, rather than a separate title slide.
- 45 seconds: founder remains the story's anchor.
- About 50 to 69 seconds: product reveal hands off to a visible terminal comparison.
- 89 seconds: full-frame type simplifies the explanation.
- 114 seconds: restrained diagram and typography explain the product category.
- 137 seconds: founder returns with a playful wardrobe change.
- 172 seconds: a branded disclaimer supplies the ending's joke.

Take for ContextTrail: explain the problem first, identify the builder, then show observable product evidence. Preserve personality through the paper-and-source concept. Do not imitate Jev's performance claims or infer why its view count is high.

## Berlin's article and Pocketsflow

[Berlin's linked article](https://x.com/berlified/status/2106832656067625025) was read in the browser. Its useful direction is an opening the viewer can understand, a coherent visual idea, satisfying movement, and a reason to continue.

[Chain's 15-second Pocketsflow piece](https://x.com/achxvi/status/2103918792845963545) was inspected at opening, about 3, 7 and 12 seconds, and in playback. It uses a recurring dithered character, abrupt white/blue/black field changes, large type, product cards and a phone payoff. The character changes jobs across the film and supplies continuity.

Take: give ContextTrail one recurring object, an evidence sheet and the line connecting it to its source. Make the important UI action change the composition. Avoid unrelated camera drift.

## Chosen direction

Three concepts considered: a detective board, a browser-window chase, and an evidence sheet that assembles a trail. The evidence sheet best fits the actual authored UI and the real source-inspection workflow. The opening uses faster cuts; the inspected passage gets a readable hold; the ending gathers the trail into the ContextTrail mark.

The film uses actual production-app captures and the supplied local video, local generated narration, an original synthesized score and restrained interface sounds. It starts with the problem, contains the exact requested Vasu introduction, and shows retained real investigations rather than fictional walkthrough data.
