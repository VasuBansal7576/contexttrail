## Motion and interaction decisions

- Move the viewer's attention from the question to the discovered source, then to the retained passage. Each camera move or reveal must explain that relationship.
- Keep quotations, dates and assessment text still while they are read. Adjust hold lengths using the rendered film at its intended viewing size.
- Use brief motion between decisions and longer pauses on evidence. Avoid an identical animation recipe in every chapter.
- Make the engraved hand point at the exact source, quotation or next action under discussion. Verify its target in every final frame size.
- In the companion, let the viewer pause on a source, read its retained text and deliberately open the original. An attractive transition must not remove inspection time.
- Choose the final invitation around an action the viewer can perform immediately. Show the actual question entry rather than a decorative closing screen.
