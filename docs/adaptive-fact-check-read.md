# Bounded alternate fact-check acquisition

After the first four deep reads, an eligible inspected historical source link still
gets first priority for the final read. Otherwise, a frozen fact-check winner that
failed to fetch, reached a rejected source binding, or yielded empty readable text
can allow an alternate historical fact-check to replace the fifth planned read.
The fifth slot must be undated-core recovery or ordinary filler. Protected anchor,
conflict, support, fact-check, current-reporting and historical-cue slots are preserved.
An unattempted/aborted winner, extraction exception or usable text does not trigger
this path. Deadline and cancellation checks still govern dispatch.

The alternate must already be retained and judged, have a safe source URL and a
bounded historical-media title/snippet cue, and not name a resource already requested
(including conservatively normalized URLs). Semantic query differences remain distinct.
It uses the existing fact-check comparator: document-role probability, relevance,
provider rank and ID. A new acquisition guard requires its fact-check probability
to be at least the failed winner's frozen probability. This signal is a model's
document-role assessment, not authority or truth. The original fact-check category
has no relevance minimum; this alternate category can therefore acquire a `.68`
article without lowering the separate `.70` historical-cue gate or any final
relevance, media-identity, date or corroboration requirement.

This trades one otherwise planned recovery/filler inspection for another article
inspection. The alternate can fail too. It adds no search, candidate or sixth read,
and links found on the fifth article remain inspectable with `page_limit`. Acquiring
article text, recovering an explicit original reference, binding submitted media,
and establishing a dated occurrence remain separate outcomes. An article's publication
date is not transferred to its linked video.

`sourceLinkedReport.readSelectionAudit` records primitive pre-read relevance and
fact-check probabilities, the fact-check winner flag, bounded historical cue, and
planned reason/position for safe retained references. Its final decision records
original-plan, inspected-link, alternate-fact-check, deadline or no-final-page choice
separately. Later classification cannot rewrite these frozen scores. Unsafe references
are counted as withheld; their URLs are not stored and remaining positions are not
renumbered. The browser boundary checks bounds, finite probabilities, enums, unique
IDs/positions and source ownership against the original frame snapshot. An alternate
decision must also match its declared failed winner, role guard and unprotected
displaced slot. A decision referencing a withheld source is withheld from the
validated projection rather than inventing a binding. Missing, malformed and legacy
diagnostics leave the otherwise valid completed/saved report readable. The exact
raw archive remains inspectable; case edits/revert do not rewrite its original trace.
No provider text or HTML is added to the diagnostic.
