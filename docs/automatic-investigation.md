# Automatic investigations

The primary paths are `/questions`, `/investigate` and `/video`. Manual evidence entry is optional. The cover follows the authored “A Place for the Question” reference.

Questions use up to six bounded searches, retain at most twelve sources and read up to ten HTML or selectable-text PDF pages. Initial searches preserve the original question; a nonempty first pass receives three additional searches for distinct evidence goals. Up to two explicit article references are followed within the same read/source allocation. Profile, sign-up and hashtag links cannot qualify merely through research wording in their surrounding paragraph. The result organises exact retained passages into explanations, measurements, history and limitations, or the relevant review, brand, attribution or shopping facets. Missing facets remain open. Choose “Check a claim” to compare the exact assertion with the retained passages. The `Claim:` prefix is also supported for pasted input; a broad question remains a broad question. No invented user claim or unsourced generated answer is substituted.

Topic searches run in two batches of at most three independent requests. Each request has a 60-second limit, matching the [official SerpApi JavaScript integration's default](https://serpapi.com/integrations/javascript). Results merge in search-plan order, preserving duplicate ownership and date observations regardless of completion order. A failed request does not trigger a retry. Cancellation waits for all dispatched requests to settle before releasing the shared usage lease. Image investigations retain their 12-second search and 55-second overall limits. Topic and watch checks have a five-minute overall limit; audio allows eight minutes and video twelve, including bounded local decoding and spoken-source research. These are cutoffs, not expected run times or additional provider allowance.

The document reader also recognises a bounded static LinkedIn short-link notice and follows its unique, explicitly displayed HTTPS article destination. It uses the existing three-hop redirect cap, five-second deadline, byte cap and public-DNS pinned transport. Ambiguous, mismatched, credential-bearing or private destinations are not followed. No scripts execute. Destination passages and dates belong to the destination URL. This support does not infer redirects from ordinary article links or recover authentication-gated pages.

Video research locally decodes the full track into two 64×64 luminance samples per second, records pixel changes, and selects up to three web-search frames with beginning/end coverage and an observed change or middle sample. Fine detail and brief events can be missed. Local supplied-media matching retains its separately evaluated uniform sampling method. Distinct web-search frames are investigated independently. Caption mode reserves at most 24 searches, 3 uploads, 192 Jev requests and 876 questions; trace mode reserves 18 / 3 / 192 / 399. These ceilings include one separately reported spoken-source investigation. They are worst-case reservations, not actual provider usage. Per-frame offsets are not publication dates; even verified stills cannot establish whole-video identity, continuity or authenticity.

Audio and video speech is decoded locally by official `whisper.cpp` using the pinned multilingual small model. Inputs are bounded to 32 MiB / 120 seconds, decoded to mono 16 kHz PCM and recognized on CPU. A configured model is verified by SHA-256 before use. Original audio is never sent to a speech API. Each segment retains its media-relative offsets and recognition status; no speaker identity or authenticity is inferred. Repeated dominant recognition marks all segments uncertain and prevents automatic speech queries. Usable speech generates one bounded source-research query, explicitly labeled unreviewed. All usable segments are included when they fit; longer transcripts use distributed first/middle/end leads. The complete recognition stays inspectable independently of this partial search wording. Existing saved reports retain their historical query verbatim. For audio, a supplied claim can still be researched when speech recognition is unusable. Completed spoken evidence survives visual decoding or retrieval failure. Missing decoders and recognition failures produce visible uncertainty, not replacement text.

Run `node scripts/setup-local-speech.mjs` with trusted FFmpeg/FFprobe and `whisper-cli` installed. The installer downloads the [official pinned model](https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp/blob/90a64d80ea254cf67575b41a5971f972c79f7b45/ggml-small.bin), verifies hash `1be3a9b2063867b937e64e2ec7483364a79917e157fa98c5d94b5c1fffea987b`, and configures its ignored local path. It preserves provider configuration and changes no account or billing settings. The model is about 488 MB; it must not be committed.

`/families` automatically compares inspected statements across the newest twenty saved cases. Matching retained wording, plausible connections and same-page wording variants receive different labels. Rechecks of identical page wording are merged, with each saved reference's publication/retrieval dates preserved. Source snippets do not enter the groups. Publication order is the order of this retrieved sample; direction of spread, authorship and independence require their own evidence. The view is bounded to 400 distinct statements and twenty connected groups.

Both paths keep completed investigations in the local casebook by default. Uncheck “Keep this investigation in my casebook” to opt out. Retained reports exclude original uploads and frame bytes. Reopening performs no provider retrieval. Source edits invalidate stale derived reports; historical revisions remain available.

HTML/PDF source text is untrusted material. PDFs are read in an isolated bounded worker, with at most twelve pages / 64,000 characters; OCR, figures and scanned pages are unsupported. Page-reading failure preserves a search lead with its original attribution. A search snippet never silently becomes a page quote.

Real 4 October UI runs exposed both selection failures and useful recovered evidence. The expanded Delhi run scanned 121 local samples, searched 0 / 10.5 / 60 second targets, retained uncertain speech without querying it, and recovered a provider-reported exact sampled-frame occurrence from Newschecker. Its first-frame result was a possible context conflict, rather than whole-video identity. Source recovery also reproduced a concrete profile/sign-up selection bug (#56), now covered by a regression test; a real recheck remains required. See the [current specification](../contexttrail_master_product_ux_architecture_spec_v1.1.md) and [product work log](product/work-log.md).

For ongoing retrieval, see [automatic watches](automatic-watches.md). Keys, finite provider allowance and single-host persistence are required for live paths. Keyless previews do not contact providers.
